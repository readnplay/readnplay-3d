Stamp Organizer Board 25 mm + Ink Pad Slot

Every 25 x 25 mm stamp gets its own pocket, and the 12.3 cm 5-color ink pad sits in front (Read and Play compatible).

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Pockets 26.4 mm for stamps with a 25 x 25 mm base, 8 mm deep – works for any stamp height
  • 30 pockets (Korean set), 36 pockets (alphabet set) or 12 pockets
  • Ink pad slot 124.5 x 24.5 mm for a 2.3 x 12.3 cm pad, with a finger notch
  • Print the small fit test first – it takes a few minutes and tells you which slot width suits your printer

Try it like this

  • Put each stamp back so it is easy to find next time
  • Ink pad and stamps together on one board

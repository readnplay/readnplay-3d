Seed Starter Tray with Water Tray + Plant Labels

Start seeds on the windowsill. Every cell has a drain hole and the tray sits on rails in its water tray.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 2x3 cells of 50 mm, 3x4 cells of 40 mm, 4x6 cells of 38 mm – 45 mm deep
  • Water trays with rails that lift the cells 3 mm
  • Plant labels 80 and 120 mm with a writing area
  • PETG recommended for long contact with water

Try it like this

  • Start lettuce and basil in spring
  • Write the sowing date on the label

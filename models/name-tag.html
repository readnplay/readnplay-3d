Initial Bag Tags A–Z & Korean + Inside Card Tag

Mark school bags without showing the full name: an initial tag outside, the name card inside.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Initial tags A–Z and Korean initials ㄱ–ㅎ with a strap slot
  • Inside-bag card tag: slide in a paper name card (50 x 30 or 86 x 54 mm) and hang it inside the bag
  • Tip: a full name visible on the outside lets strangers call your child by name – keep it inside
  • Raised letter – change filament at the letter layer for two colors

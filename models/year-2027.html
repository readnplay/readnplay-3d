2027 New Year Number Stand

Ring in 2027. Free-standing numbers on a small base – print it in gold, silver or silk.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 40 mm tall (155 mm wide) or 65 mm tall (242 mm wide)

Gridfinity-Compatible Bins – 17 Sizes, Magnets

Bins that drop into any standard 42 mm Gridfinity baseplate, with a stacking lip.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 1x1, 1x2, 1x3, 2x2, 2x3, 3x3 – heights 3u (21 mm) and 6u (42 mm)
  • Magnet-hole versions (6 x 2 mm magnets) for 1x1, 1x2, 2x2
  • 2- and 3-compartment versions
  • Baseplates are a separate model (search my profile)
  • Gridfinity is an open standard by Zack Freedman – this is an independent compatible design

Jegi Base & Spinning Tops – Korean Folk Games

Two classic Korean folk toys. Make a jegi with a printed base and paper or plastic-bag strips, and spin a hand top or a whip top.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Jegi base 40 or 46 mm: fold plastic or paper strips, push them into the hole and lock them with the tapered pin
  • Pocket underneath for two 500-won coins (tape them in) for a nicer kick
  • Hand tops 40 and 55 mm with a press-in stem; whip top 55 mm with three string grooves
  • Balance checked: the centre of mass sits exactly on the axis, and every top is wider than tall in inertia (Izz/Ixx 1.4–1.6) so it spins steadily
  • Tops print upside down (flat face on the bed) – no supports
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Contains small parts – for ages 6 and up.

Try it like this

  • Make a jegi from strips of a plastic bag
  • Who can keep the top spinning longest?

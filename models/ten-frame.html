Ten Frame, Five Frame & 20 Frame + 35 mm Counters

The classic counting tool: put a counter in each cell to count, add and make 10.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 5-frame, 10-frame and 20-frame with 40 mm cells
  • Counters 35 mm wide with a finger ring on top
  • Two counter plates so you can print two colors (e.g. red and yellow)
  • Prints flat in one piece – no supports
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.

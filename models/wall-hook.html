Adhesive Wall Hooks – S / M / L, Strong Print

Stick-on hooks for keys, towels and bags. Printed on their side so the layers follow the curve of the hook.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • S 12 mm wide (keys, earphones)
  • M 20 mm (towels, caps)
  • L 30 mm (bags, coats)
  • Flat back for double-sided tape (3M VHB)
  • Load depends on your tape and wall – start light

Print settings

Material
PLA (PETG also fine)

0.20 mm

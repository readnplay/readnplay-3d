New Year Money Envelope Stand & Lucky Pouch

Get ready for Seollal: stand the money envelopes up for the bow ceremony, and keep coins in a lucky-pouch shaped box.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Envelope stand with 3 or 5 slots, leaning 15° back so envelopes stay put
  • Lucky pouch (bokjumeoni) coin box, 104 mm wide, prints without supports (all overhangs 45° or less)
  • Lid with a 56 x 4.5 mm slot – also fits the 200 % play coins (500-won play coin 53 mm)
  • Separate knot decoration to glue on top – print it in a second color
  • "새해 복 많이 받으세요" on the stand, "복" on the lid

Try it like this

  • Line up the envelopes before the New Year bow
  • Save the coins children receive in the lucky pouch

School Set – Pencil Clips, Timetable Stand, Hooks

Small helpers for the first school year: initial clips so pencils do not get mixed up, a timetable stand and a hook for the shoe bag.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Pencil clips with initials A–Z and Korean ㄱ–ㅎ, plus blank clips for a name sticker (pencil 7.6 mm, thick pencil 10 mm)
  • Timetable stand for A5 cards, portrait (150) or landscape (215), with a pencil groove
  • Desk-side hook for the shoe bag – clamps on 18, 22 or 25 mm desks, no screws
  • S-hook for a backpack strap
  • Tip: initials only on the outside – keep the full name inside the bag
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Contains small parts – for ages 6 and up.

Try it like this

  • Clip an initial on every pencil so they do not get mixed up in class
  • Check tomorrow's things on the timetable stand

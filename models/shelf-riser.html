Cabinet Shelf Riser – Double Your Shelf Space

Add a second level inside a cabinet for cups, spice jars or canned food.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • W200 x D100 x H80, W200 x D130 x H120, W150 x D100 x H80 mm
  • Prints upside down – no supports
  • Ribs under the top for stiffness; open side frames to reach in
  • For light kitchen items – not for heavy pots

Try it like this

  • Spice jars above the cups in a cabinet
  • Use the space above slippers in a shoe cabinet

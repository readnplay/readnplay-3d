Battery Organizer – AA, AAA, 9V, CR2032, 18650

Keep spare batteries upright so you can see at a glance what is left.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • AA 9 + AAA 16
  • AA 20
  • AAA 24
  • Combo: AA 8 + AAA 8 + 9V 2 + CR2032 6
  • 18650 x 12
  • Chamfered openings, labels on top
  • Tip: fresh batteries + side up, used ones – side up

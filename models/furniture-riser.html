Heavy-Duty Furniture & Washer Riser – Stackable

Lift a sofa, bed, cabinet or washing machine. Each riser has a 46 mm cup for the foot; choose the one-piece version for heavy loads or stack tiers to set the height. Two print profiles: PLA and PETG.

Features

  • Which version? PLA: furniture that does not vibrate, indoors at room temperature. PETG: washing machines and dryers, near heaters, sunny balconies in summer (PLA softens from about 50–55 °C)
  • The PLA version has its own shape for a brittle material: no grooves on the outside, 3 mm cup reinforcement, larger chamfers, 0.5 mm chamfer at the pin roots
  • One-piece: 65 mm wide, 86 mm tall – the strongest option. Stackable: base 14 + mid tiers 24 + top 24 mm = 38, 62, 86 or 110 mm; the 5 mm pins only align, the flat faces carry the load
  • PETG profile: PETG HF, 0.20 mm Strength, 6 walls, 6/5 top/bottom, 40 % gyroid – one-piece about 166 g, 5 h 40 min (P1S)
  • PLA profile: PLA Basic at 230 °C for better layer bonding, 0.20 mm Strength, 6 walls, 6/5 top/bottom, 50 % gyroid – one-piece about 193 g, 7 h 36 min
  • Lighter settings (4 walls, 25 %) are fine for light furniture only
  • Printed upright as used: the load presses the layers together. No supports, no overhangs. Textured PEI plate
  • Always use four risers of the same height on a flat floor. Load capacity depends on your print and was not lab-tested – check after the first use, and for washing machines after the first spin cycle
  • No anti-slip rubber is included – add felt or rubber pads if your floor is smooth

Try it like this

  • Raise a bed for more storage – PLA is fine indoors, four at the same height
  • Lift a washer for easier loading – use PETG and check after the first spin

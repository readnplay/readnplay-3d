Drawer Organizer Trays – 40 mm Grid, 14 Sizes

Mix and match trays on a 40 mm grid to fill any drawer. Rounded inside corners make it easy to scoop small parts.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 1x1, 1x2, 1x3, 2x2, 2x3, 2x4, 3x3 grid cells
  • Heights 25 and 40 mm
  • Each tray is 0.5 mm smaller than the grid so they drop in side by side

Try it like this

  • Measure the drawer and combine grid trays
  • Divide a kitchen drawer for utensils

Pattern Blocks & Tangram – 25 / 40 mm Sets

Six classic shapes that fit together (hexagon, trapezoid, two rhombuses, triangle, square) and a 7-piece tangram with a frame.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Pattern blocks with 25 mm or 40 mm sides – 23 pieces per set
  • Every shape shares the same side length, so they tile perfectly (2 trapezoids = 1 hexagon, 3 triangles = 1 trapezoid)
  • Tangram 100 or 150 mm with a frame tray
  • Print each shape in its own color – one plate per color group
  • 0.15 mm clearance and rounded corners for easy fitting
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Contains small parts – for ages 6 and up.

Try it like this

  • Two trapezoids make a hexagon, three triangles a trapezoid
  • Make animals with the 7 tangram pieces

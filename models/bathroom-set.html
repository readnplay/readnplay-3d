Bathroom Set – Toothbrush, Razor & Squeezer

Wall-mounted holders that stick on with tape – the holes go all the way through so water never collects.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Toothbrush holder for 2, 4 or 6 brushes (14 mm holes)
  • Razor holder for 2 razors with open slots
  • Toothpaste squeezer for 50 and 65 mm wide tubes
  • 45° shelf underneath – prints upright without supports
  • PETG recommended in the bathroom (PLA softens with heat and moisture)

Try it like this

  • Family toothbrushes off the counter and dripping dry
  • Squeeze the tube to the very end

Fraction Circles & Bars – 1 to 1/12 with Trays

See and feel fractions: put two 1/4 pieces on a 1/2, or line up bars to compare 1/3 and 1/4.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Circles 80 mm or 120 mm: 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/6, 1/8, 1/10, 1/12
  • Bars 160 mm in 9 rows with a tray that keeps every row in place
  • Every piece is labeled with raised numbers
  • Tip: print each denominator in its own color – one plate per color, no AMS needed
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Contains small parts – for ages 6 and up.

Try it like this

  • Share a pizza: compare 1/2 and 1/4
  • Line up bars: which is bigger, 1/3 or 1/4?

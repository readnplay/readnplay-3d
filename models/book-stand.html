Book Stand S / M / L + Page Holders

Read hands-free. A sturdy stand for paperbacks, books and textbooks, plus a thumb page holder that keeps pages open.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • S: width 120, slot 25 mm (paperback) · M: width 160, slot 35 mm · L: width 200, slot 45 mm (textbook, sheet music)
  • Back angle 65° – or M at 55° for reading at a desk
  • Printed on its side so the layers run along the stand
  • Page holder rings for small, medium and large thumbs

Try it like this

  • Keep a cookbook open while cooking
  • Sheet music or textbooks upright for better posture

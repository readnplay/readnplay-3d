Snap-In Cable Clips – 3 to 12 mm, 1 to 4 Slots

Clean up the cables on your desk. Push the cable in – it clicks and stays.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Single clips: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 mm cable diameter
  • Multi clips: 2 x 4, 2 x 5, 3 x 4, 3 x 5, 3 x 6, 4 x 5 mm
  • Screw-tab versions if tape does not hold on your surface
  • Opening = 70 % of the cable diameter, 0.5 mm clearance inside
  • Flat base for double-sided tape (3M VHB works well)
  • PETG is a bit more forgiving than PLA for clips
  • Measure your cable – if it is between sizes, take the bigger clip

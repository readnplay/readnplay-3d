Learning Clock – Korean or English Face, 2 Sizes

Learn to tell time with movable hands. The Korean face shows minutes as 5분, 10분 … and says which hand is which.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Faces 150 mm and 200 mm, Korean or English
  • Hour numbers 1–12 and minute labels every 5 minutes, 60 tick marks
  • Hour and minute hands turn on a printed pin with a press-on cap
  • Stand included, or hang it on the wall
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Contains small parts – for ages 6 and up.

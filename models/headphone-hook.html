Under-Desk Headphone Hook – Screw, Tape or Clamp

Hang your headphones under the desk. Choose how you mount it: screws, tape, or a clamp that slides onto the desk edge.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Screw mount 36 mm wide, and 50 mm for wide gaming headsets
  • Tape mount 50 mm with a shallow recess for the tape
  • Desk clamp – no screws, no tape – for desks 16, 18, 25, 30, 40 mm thick
  • All printed on their side for strength. PETG recommended for the clamp

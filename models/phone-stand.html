Phone & Tablet Stand – 4 Angles + Thick Case

A simple, strong desk stand with a slot for the charging cable. Printed on its side so the layers run along the stand.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Phone 65° – fits phones up to 13 mm thick (with case)
  • Phone 65° thick case – up to 17 mm (rugged cases)
  • Phone 55° (reading on the desk) and 75° (video calls)
  • Tablet 65° – up to 15 mm thick, 140 mm wide
  • 18 mm slot in the front lip for the charging cable
  • Works in portrait and landscape

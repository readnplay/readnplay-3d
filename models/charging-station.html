Charging Station for Phones & Power Banks

Park phones, power banks and a tablet upright. Each slot has a cable hole and the cables run out the back underneath.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 3 slots (14 / 14 / 10 mm), 4 slots (14 / 14 / 14 / 20 mm) or 2 wide slots (20 / 20 mm)
  • Cable channel underneath, exits at the back
  • Slots are 34 mm deep – devices stand on their own

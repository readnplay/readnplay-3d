Small Parts Organizer with Lid – 4 to 24 Cells

Screws, beads, SD cards, LEGO – a compartment box with a press-fit lid so nothing mixes.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 2x2, 3x2 (35 mm tall), 4x2, 4x3 and 6x4 cells of 30 mm
  • Press-fit lid with 0.2 mm clearance and a finger notch
  • Dividers stop below the lid, so the lid sits flat

Try it like this

  • Sort small LEGO pieces

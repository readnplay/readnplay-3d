Perpetual Desk Calendar – Korean & English Tiles

A desk calendar you never have to buy again. Swap the month, date and weekday tiles each day – in Korean or English.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Stand 200 mm wide with a leaning back and a front lip
  • Number tiles 0–3 and 0–9 for every date
  • Korean tiles (1월–12월, 월요일–일요일) and English tiles (JAN–DEC, MON–SUN)
  • Raised text – change filament at the text layer for two colors
  • Tile box with three compartments for the spare tiles
  • Korean text uses Noto Sans KR (SIL Open Font License)

Try it like this

  • Change the date with your child every morning
  • Learn weekday and month names with English tiles

Yutnori Set – Sticks, Board, Pieces & Box

The traditional Korean New Year game. Throw four sticks and race your pieces around the board – pieces stack when they travel together.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Half-round sticks 100 or 150 mm, flat side down – one stick has an X on the flat side for back-do (뒷도)
  • Board 230 x 230 mm in two halves that join with three dovetails – fits a 256 mm bed
  • 29 stations with recessed spots, bigger spots at the corners and the centre
  • 4 teams x 4 pieces, 34 mm wide (above the 31.7 mm small-parts size), marked with 1–4 dots
  • Pieces stack (업기): a dome on top fits the recess underneath
  • Storage box with lid for sticks, pieces and the folded board halves
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.

Try it like this

  • Family yutnori at Seollal – stack and capture
  • Keep the board halves in the box until the next holiday

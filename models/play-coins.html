Korean Won Play Coins 160% / 200% + Tray

Play money coins for learning Korean won (not legal tender, 160% / 200% scale). Count, swap and play shop with 10, 50, 100 and 500 won.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Set A 160%: 28.8 / 34.6 / 38.4 / 42.4 mm · Set B 200%: 36 / 43.2 / 48 / 53 mm – same size ratio as real coins
  • Own design only: big number, "won", a simple symbol (star, leaf, flower, sun). Back: "놀이용 PLAY" so they are never mixed up with real money
  • Feel the difference: 10 smooth edge, 50 eight notches, 100 fine ridges, 500 big ridges
  • Two colors without AMS: change filament at the top layer (copper for 10, silver/grey for the others)
  • Coin tray with a well for each coin, stack tubes that hold exactly 10 coins (10 x 10 = 100, 10 x 50 = 500) and price-tag holders for playing shop
  • Made larger than real coins and in plastic on purpose (Bank of Korea rule: 150 % or more, no metal). Please do not scale them down to real size or make them in metal
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.
  • Set A 10-won coin is smaller than 31.7 mm – use Set B with younger children

Try it like this

  • Play shop: put on price tags and pay with coins
  • Fill a stack tube: 10 x 10 won = 100 won
  • Close your eyes and guess the coin by its edge

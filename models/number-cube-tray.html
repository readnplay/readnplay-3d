Number Rod Cube Tray – Rods 1 to 9 (18.2 mm)

A tray for the cubes that go with an 18.2 mm number board (Read and Play compatible): a groove for every rod length 2–9 and a cell for the single cubes.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Grooves 19.6 mm wide, lengths = number of cubes x 18.2 mm, numbered 2–9
  • Big cell for 30 single cubes
  • Cube height was not available, so grooves are 14 mm deep with room for fingers
  • Print the small fit test first – it takes a few minutes and tells you which slot width suits your printer
  • Not a certified toy. Not for children under 3. Use with adult supervision. Do not put in mouth.

Try it like this

  • Put the rods back by length after playing
  • Guess the rod length from the groove

Self-Watering Planter – S / M / L, Wick System

Water once a week instead of every day. A cotton wick pulls water from the reservoir into the soil.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Each size = pot + reservoir on one plate
  • Notch in the rim to pour water and check the level
  • Center hole for an 8–10 mm cotton rope wick + 4 drain holes
  • PETG recommended for long-term water contact. Not for food use.

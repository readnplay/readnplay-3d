Magnetic Number Organizer 0–9 + Bars & Signs

A box for foam magnetic number sets (Read and Play compatible): one cell for each digit 0–9, a long cell for number bars and a cell for small squares.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Digit cells 30 x 40 mm for digits up to 25 x 36 mm, numbers engraved in the bottom
  • Long cell 158 mm for bars up to 150 mm
  • Separate cells for signs (+ − =) and 15 mm squares
  • One piece, prints without supports

Try it like this

  • Learn the order 0–9 while putting numbers back
  • Take out bars and numbers to compare amounts

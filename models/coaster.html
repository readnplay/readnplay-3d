Coaster Set with Holder – Hex & Round

A simple coaster set with a raised ring pattern and a holder for six.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • Hex 95 mm or round 100 mm, 5 mm thick
  • Holder for 6 coasters with a finger cut-out
  • Great in two colors (AMS color change at the pattern layer)
  • Print in TPU for a non-slip coaster

Print settings

Material
PLA (PETG also fine)

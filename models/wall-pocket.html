Wall Pocket for Remotes, Glasses & Keys

A slim wall pocket for the things that always go missing: remotes, glasses, keys, mail.

MakerWorld – coming soon

Free for personal use. The download page has the ready-to-print Bambu Studio file (one plate per size) and the STL files.

Features

  • 1, 2 or 3 pockets, 30 mm deep – or 2 deep pockets (50 mm) for glasses cases
  • Low front with a finger cut-out so you can see and grab things
  • Two countersunk screw holes – or use double-sided tape

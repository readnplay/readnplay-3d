About

Read & Play 3D is a small studio that makes useful 3D print models for everyday life – things that hold, sort and organize. We focus on what people actually ask for: the right size, a good fit, and no supports.

How the models are made

Every model is a parametric CAD design, which is why each one comes in many sizes. We use AI-assisted tools in our design workflow, and every plate is sliced and checked in Bambu Studio before release.

License

The models are free for personal use. Please do not sell the files or prints made from them without asking. Sharing a link to this site or to the download page is always welcome.

Requests

Need a size that is not here? Leave a comment on the model's download page and we will try to add it.

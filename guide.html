Print guide

Every model on this site is made so you can print it without supports on a normal 0.4 mm nozzle. Here is how to get the right size the first time.

1. Measure first

Measure the thing you want to store or hold – the cable diameter, the phone with its case, the desk thickness. If you are between two sizes, take the bigger one.

2. One plate = one size

The Bambu Studio file on the download page has one plate per size, each with a clear name. Open the file, click the plate you want, and press Print plate. You do not need to print every plate.

3. Lids too tight or too loose?

Every printer is a little different. Boxes with lids come with a tight, normal and loose lid, plus a small fit test piece. Print the test first – it takes a few minutes and saves a whole box.

4. Recommended settings

PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 2 walls, 15 % infill. Clips and hooks are a bit tougher in PETG. Hooks are printed on their side so the layers run along the load.

5. Not a Bambu Lab printer?

The STL files work in any slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer). Place them flat as they come – they are already in the right print orientation.
